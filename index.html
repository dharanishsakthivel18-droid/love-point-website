FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Write to us anytime.

What is LOVE POINT?

A private app for two people with one-to-one chat, a shared Memory Wall and more. It is not a social media or dating app.

What is a LOVE POINT ID?

A permanent 6-digit number given to you at sign up. Share it with your partner so they can find you. It never changes.

Who can see my chats and memories?

Only you and the person you share them with. Chats are private to the two people in them, and a Memory Wall is private to the two connected partners.

Is it free?

Yes, LOVE POINT is free to download and use.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. LOVE POINT is currently available for Android.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account inside the app and choose Remove Account. This permanently deletes your account.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

LOVE POINT ("we", "our") is a private messaging and memory app for two people. This policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
  • Profile: your LOVE POINT ID and optional profile photo.
  • Content you create: chat messages, photos and videos you send, and memories (photo, title, note).
  • Notification data: in-app notifications, your notification preferences and a device push token.
  • Basic technical data: app version and device platform, only when you contact support.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and let you chat privately with your partner.
  • To store and show your memories to you and your connected partner.
  • To send you notifications you have chosen to receive.
  • To provide support and keep the app secure.

3. Who can see your content

Chat messages are visible only to the two participants. A Memory Wall is visible only to the two partners who connected it. Photos, videos and profile pictures are kept in private storage. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

4. Where data is stored

Your data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, using access rules that restrict each piece of data to the people it belongs to.

5. Permissions

  • Photos / media: to choose pictures and videos to send or save as memories.
  • Camera: to take a profile picture.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new messages and memories.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can delete your own messages and memories, change your name and password, turn notification types on or off, sign out from all devices, and permanently remove your account from inside the app. You may also contact us to request deletion.

7. Security

We use access rules, private storage and secure connections to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and never share it.

8. Children

LOVE POINT is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
